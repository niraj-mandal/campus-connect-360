Help Desk

Hostel repairs, Wi-Fi trouble, fee questions: raise a ticket, get an ID, and follow it until it’s resolved.

Before you raise a ticket

Quick fixes

IT & Wi-Fi

Can’t connect to campus Wi-Fi

Forget the “Campus-Secure” network, reconnect with your student ID and portal password, and accept the certificate. Still failing? Raise a ticket with your device type.

Fees

Fee paid but not showing

Payments take up to 2 working days to reflect. After that, raise a ticket with the transaction ID and date of payment.

Hostel

Room repair or electrical fault

Raise a Hostel ticket with your block and room number. Electrical and water issues are attended first, usually within 24 hours.

Library

Late fine or lost book

Fines can be paid at the circulation desk. For a lost book, raise a Library ticket before the due date to avoid extra charges.

Academics

Attendance or marks mismatch

Raise an Academics ticket with the course code and the dates or marks in question. Your department office will review it.

Urgent

Medical or safety emergency

Don’t wait for a ticket. Call campus security on Ext. 2000 or go to the Medical Centre, open 24 hours.
