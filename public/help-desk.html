Help Desk

Hostel repairs, Wi-Fi trouble, fee questions: raise a ticket, get an ID, and follow it until it’s resolved.

Before you raise a ticket

Quick fixes

IT & Wi-Fi

Can’t connect to campus Wi-Fi

Forget the campus Wi-Fi network on your device and reconnect with your university login. Still failing? Raise a ticket with your device type and hostel or block.

Fees

Fee paid but not showing

Online payments can take a little time to reflect. If it still isn’t showing, raise a ticket with the transaction ID and payment date, or call the LPU helpline on +91 1824 517000.

Hostel

Room repair or electrical fault

Raise a Hostel ticket with your hostel, block and room number, and tell your hostel warden. Mention if it’s an electrical or water issue so it’s prioritised.

Library

Late fine or lost book

Ask at the library circulation desk about fines. For a lost book, raise a Library ticket before the due date.

Academics

Attendance or marks mismatch

Raise an Academics ticket with the course code and the dates or marks in question. Your department office will review it.

Urgent

Medical or safety emergency

Don’t wait for a ticket. Go to Uni Hospital on campus (24×7 medical help), call 112 for emergencies, or the LPU helpline +91 1824 517000.
